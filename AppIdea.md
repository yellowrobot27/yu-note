### The App

- **__YuNote__** is a personal Mandarin-learning application that turns everyday notes into personalized learning material. Users can write notes about their daily lives, studies, interests, or anything else they want to remember. From the content of these notes, the app automatically suggests useful words and expressions to learn in Mandarin.

- For each suggestion, the user can access information such as the Chinese characters, pinyin, meaning, pronunciation, and example sentences. If the user decides to learn an item, it is saved together with the original note, allowing them to review it later in the context in which they encountered it.

- The main idea is to combine personal note-taking with vocabulary acquisition and spaced repetition, helping users build their Mandarin knowledge through content that is relevant to them personally.

### Target Audience

The primary target audience is beginner and intermediate Mandarin learners who want to expand their vocabulary and retain what they learn. Rather than following a fixed curriculum, users build a personalized collection of vocabulary based on their own lives and interests.

### What am I going to build? (Roadmap)

- **__Basic application__**: User authentication and the ability to create, edit, view, and delete personal notes.
- **__Mandarin learning integration__**: Automatically suggest a small number of potentially useful words or expressions from each note. Users can choose which suggestions they want to learn.
- **__Learning details__**: For each selected item, provide its Chinese translation, pinyin, meaning, pronunciation, and example sentences, using external language resources where appropriate.
- **__Review system__**: Introduce quizzes and spaced repetition to help users remember previously learned vocabulary. Reviews could include recognizing meanings, recalling translations, and completing sentences.
- **__Additional features__**: Offline support, notifications, accessibility features.

### 4. Why this app?

The main point of the app is the connection between a vocabulary item and the personal context in which it was encountered. This could make learning more relevant and memorable while reducing the effort required to create study material manually.

### 5. Competitors

Competitors come in different categories: Curriculum apps (Duolingo, HelloChinese), SRS Flashcard & Note Apps (Anki, RemNote) and Contextual Readers (Du Chinese, Chairman’s Bao), but by leveraging it's advantages (zero-effort vocabulary building, personal relevance and contextual recall), YuNote can find it's own niche.
